EasyPass is installed. One last step: close Chrome and open it again.

Chrome asks "New extension added (EasyPass)" at the top right. Press "Enable extension". Done:
open a site's login page and the panel opens by itself.

If the question doesn't appear, open Chrome menu → Extensions → Manage Extensions and turn EasyPass on.

On first start, create a new vault in EasyPass or open your existing one (vault.ezp).
