EasyPass is installed. One last step: add the Chrome extension (only once).

Press "Next"; if the box on the last screen is ticked, Chrome's extensions page and the extension folder open by themselves.

1. On Chrome's extensions page, turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
2. Drag the "extension" folder, selected in the window that opened, onto the Chrome page.
   (Or press "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.)
3. "EasyPass" appears in the list. Done: open a site's login page and the panel opens by itself.

Extension folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\EasyPass\extension

On first start, create a new vault in EasyPass or open your existing one (vault.ezp).
